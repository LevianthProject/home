# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are recruiters, hiring managers, founders, product leaders, technology leaders, institutions, partners, collaborators, and design or technology peers evaluating Ghazariz for product-and-technology roles or complex engagements.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio makes Ghazariz's product thinking visible. Within the first 30 seconds it should establish that he turns ambiguous product ideas into structured, usable, and buildable systems across strategy, UX, technical planning, operations, and execution. Success means visitors explore selected work, understand his decision-making approach, view a case study, and start a real conversation.

## Positioning

Ghazariz does not only design screens. He designs the system behind them: users, roles, permissions, workflows, product boundaries, business logic, technical trade-offs, and executable delivery phases.

## Operating Context

The public portfolio presents evidence-backed work spanning education platforms, parental technology, museum systems, internal performance systems, remote-team operations, and product experiments. It is a static-first public website deployed from the `LevianthProject/home` repository to GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Phase 1 includes Home, Work, About, Process, Thoughts, Resume, Contact, and full case studies for MLS, ML Space, and Centralized Museum CMS.
- The deployment path is `/home/` on GitHub Pages and cannot rely on server-only APIs, rewrites, image optimization, or form handlers.
- Critical visuals must be local. Generated diagrams or abstract project visuals must be clearly sanitized and must not masquerade as shipped product screens.
- Smooth scrolling and authored motion must preserve native scrolling, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and usable stationary states.
- Unverified dates, links, metrics, statuses, employment claims, and outcomes remain hidden or explicitly labeled.

## Product Principles

- Build trust before spectacle: every public claim must be verifiable.
- Show systems and decisions, not a gallery of disconnected screens.
- Reduce ambiguity before teams spend heavily.
- Use motion to reveal hierarchy and causality.
- Keep the first release focused and credible; defer unsupported ambition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA intent for contrast, semantic structure, keyboard operation, focus visibility, touch targets, and motion sensitivity. Essential information cannot depend on hover, animation, pointer precision, color alone, or WebGL support.
